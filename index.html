Adorno Think and Thanks

Productizing the solution to Europe’s migration crisis

Working paper based on the public note by Ludwig Adorno (@Boavista_Ludwig)
Core claim: incentives must be realigned so that the preferred outcomes become the most profitable ones.
Usually called a Think Tank. Here: Think and Thanks — gratitude for the work ahead.

The diagnosis

The current form of large-scale migration into Europe has persisted for a long time not despite opposition, but because multiple powerful groups actively benefit from it.

Employers value higher work intensity. An asylum-processing industry has emerged in which some participants earn very high incomes. Parts of the public experience certain migrants as more outgoing or socially engaging than the native population. Because of these interests and the general preference for politeness, simple deportation of large numbers of people does not happen at scale.

Therefore any serious attempt to change the trajectory must make different people earn substantially more money by producing the outcomes that are actually desired.

Three working directions

Productize the existing tools

Deportation of criminals and remigration of people who cannot support themselves or who live permanently on public transfers should be run by the free market under clear rules.

Someone who can remigrate 90 000 people per month should be able to earn up to €90 000 per month.
Deportations should be privatized. Over time, migrants themselves may run the agencies that carry them out.

Current state-run efforts are often inefficient (one plane for one person). Market prices and competition can change that. High, transparent compensation for successful high-volume outcomes is the mechanism that makes the preferred policy actually occur.

Invent additional legal products

Even after criminals are removed and those dependent on welfare are remigrated, many people will remain through work. If their eventual political participation is expected to transform the country, further instruments are required.

Possible products under discussion:

  • A high-quality, long-term working visa with no access to welfare, no voting rights, and no path to naturalization.
  • A second-class form of citizenship with the same permanent exclusions.

Without such clear, durable distinctions, political incentives will eventually lead to mass naturalization for electoral reasons.

Make the crisis legally punishable

Those who knowingly created or sustained the conditions that produced large-scale violence, deaths, and long-term fiscal and social costs should face legal consequences.

This is not primarily about revenge. It is about restoring the expectation that policy decisions with foreseeable, severe negative externalities carry personal risk for the decision-makers.

Status of this document

This is an early draft. It records a starting position rather than a fully worked-out programme. Numbers, legal designs, incentive structures, and sequencing are all open to iteration.

The Think and Thanks purpose, for the moment, is to take this line of thought and develop it through successive boards, conversations, and written refinements. The central constraint remains: any proposed solution must make the desired outcome more profitable for capable actors than the status quo.
